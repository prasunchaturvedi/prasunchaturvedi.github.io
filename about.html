About

Policy, governance and technology, from Bhubaneswar.

I was born and brought up in Odisha, and I live and work in Bhubaneswar. I trained as a computer science engineer at Utkal University and took my MBA at XIM Bhubaneswar. I began my career at GE as a product manager in commercial finance.

Since 2007 I have held technology leadership roles with one of the largest technology conglomerates. That work took me through delivery management in the UAE, business development for emerging markets, regional IT leadership for Sub-Saharan Africa, and country leadership for Qatar and Africa. I later managed a region in France and advised a CIO on strategy. Since 2022 I have led a technology business in Odisha. Across these roles I have carried P&L responsibility and worked with governments and industry bodies on three continents.

My public work runs alongside. I have taken part in Nasscom policy consultations on IT in Odisha, contributed to consultations on the state’s IT policy and worked with the World Skill Center on skill development and a Centre of Excellence. As an industry fellow I engage with think tanks on policy formulation, and I serve as Vice President of the XIMB Alumni Association (XIMBAA), Bhubaneswar Chapter.

I am a part-time PhD candidate at XIM University’s School of Governance and Public Affairs, researching digital social protection delivery platforms. My research outlook is critical realist: I want to know what works, for whom and why, so that findings can inform real policy decisions. I am also studying for an M.Tech in Cyber Security at IIT Bhubaneswar.

I am available for policy consulting where no conflict of interest arises.
