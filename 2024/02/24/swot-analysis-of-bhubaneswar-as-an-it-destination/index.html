SWOT Analysis of Bhubaneswar as an IT Destination

24 February 2024 · Prasun Chaturvedi

Published in February 2024. Policies and figures reflect that date.

Strengths

  1. Government support: Progressive policies such as the IT Policy 2022 and the Odisha Semiconductor and Fabless Policy 2023, spearheaded by the then Chief Minister, foster innovation and investment. The recent diplomatic mission to the USA showcased Odisha's potential in the electronics and IT sector, garnering international attention.
  2. Robust infrastructure: Bhubaneswar has supportive IT enclaves such as Infocity and Infovalley, enhancing its appeal to IT companies. Cognizant's large-scale facility in Bhubaneswar signifies the city's capability to accommodate significant IT ventures.
  3. Skilled talent pool: The city has gained recognition for its skilled workforce, nurtured through proactive government initiatives in IT education and skill development. The presence of consulting firms such as PwC, Deloitte and EY, along with IT majors such as Infosys, TechM and Wipro, underscores its appeal to industry leaders and indicates the availability of high-calibre talent.
  4. Cost-effective operations: Lower operational costs compared with traditional tech hubs make Bhubaneswar an attractive destination for IT ventures. Lower living costs and lighter traffic congestion provide a conducive environment for tech innovation and business growth.
  5. Strategic location: Positioned on the east coast, Bhubaneswar has historical trade and cultural relations with Southeast Asia. Better connectivity with the Middle East and within India has enhanced its attractiveness to businesses. Its coastal location, combined with proactive government initiatives, positions Bhubaneswar as a strong hub for tech ventures.

Weaknesses

  1. Limited market presence: Despite rapid growth, Bhubaneswar still lags behind established IT hubs such as Bengaluru and Hyderabad in market presence and global recognition. While hosting over 200 IT firms, it needs to enhance its visibility and reputation in the global IT landscape.
  2. Infrastructure development: Ongoing development will be necessary to support the city's expanding IT industry. Continued investment in infrastructure is crucial to sustain the growth of the ecosystem and keep it ahead of the curve.

Opportunities

  1. Semiconductor innovation: Bhubaneswar's emergence as a centre for semiconductor innovation presents significant opportunities for industry growth. Diplomatic endeavours and global collaborations have bolstered its trajectory towards becoming the Silicon Coast of India.
  2. Expansion of the IT ecosystem: With a surge in investment and new IT companies, there is ample opportunity for Bhubaneswar to diversify and expand its IT ecosystem. Over 50 new IT companies have invested in Bhubaneswar in the last four years, indicating a thriving ecosystem.

Threats

  1. Competition: Intense competition from other emerging IT destinations in India and globally threatens Bhubaneswar's position. Established hubs such as Bengaluru and Hyderabad compete to attract and retain IT investment.
  2. Technological disruption: Rapid technological change may challenge Bhubaneswar's ability to adapt. Continuous research and development is necessary to address disruption and maintain competitiveness.
  3. External factors: Economic downturns, geopolitical tensions or natural disasters could adversely affect Bhubaneswar's IT ecosystem and investment climate. The city needs resilience strategies to mitigate their impact.
  4. Policy continuity: Uncertainty over political succession could affect governance and policy direction.

I invite your thoughts, especially any data references that can further strengthen this analysis.
